Support and accessibility

Include your operating system, Honeycomb version, and what you were trying to do. For a website issue, include the page and the part you could not access. Remove private information from screenshots or logs.

Privacy and security

Use “Honeycomb privacy request” or “Honeycomb security report” as the email subject. Send a short description first and avoid including passwords, sensitive documents, or other people’s personal information. The privacy policy explains how support messages are handled.
