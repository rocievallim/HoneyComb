Privacy policy

Last updated November 8, 2026

This policy covers the Honeycomb download website and the macOS and Windows desktop apps.

Who operates Honeycomb

Rocieval is the publisher of Honeycomb in the Philippines and the contact for privacy questions about the project. Send privacy requests to princeezekiel.limboc@gmail.com.

When you visit or download

Honeycomb’s pages are static and have no forms, visitor accounts, embedded advertising, or project analytics. I do not maintain a separate database of visitors or downloads.

The server that delivers these pages and downloads may record technical information such as IP address, browser type, request time, and requested file. This information may be used for delivery, security, and troubleshooting. Retention and processing locations depend on the hosting service. Hosting and email services may process information outside the Philippines.

When you use the desktop app

Honeycomb reads files you select and uses local conversion tools to create outputs. The desktop apps have no Honeycomb upload endpoint or analytics service. Installation and dependency setup may contact package repositories or vendors. External tools and operating-system services have their own data practices.

  • macOS. Accessibility and Input Monitoring support the gesture. Finder automation reads the selected files. Local settings, history, and logs are kept in ~/honeycomb.
  • Windows. Keyboard and mouse hooks detect your configured gesture. Shell integration reads the current File Explorer or Desktop selection. Local settings, history, and logs are kept under %LOCALAPPDATA%\Honeycomb.

Local records may include file names, paths, conversion results, and errors. They stay on your device until the app replaces them or you remove them. You can pause the gesture in the app, revoke relevant macOS permissions in System Settings, and remove local records after quitting. The clipboard receives converted files only when you select that action.

When you email us

We receive your email address, message, and anything you choose to include. We use them to respond, troubleshoot, or handle a request about your data. Gmail handles delivery and storage. Send only the information needed and remove private names, paths, and file contents from logs or screenshots.

Support and privacy emails are kept while we handle the request and only as long afterward as necessary for related follow-up, a legal obligation, or an unresolved dispute. We do not use support email for marketing or sell personal data.

Why we process data and who receives it

We use the information described above to deliver and secure the requested website, respond to communications you initiate, and meet applicable legal duties. Where a legal basis is required, it depends on the activity and may include providing a service you request, legitimate interests in operating and securing it, or compliance with legal obligations. Hosting and email providers receive data needed for their respective services. We may disclose information if legally required.

Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access or correct data, object to processing, request deletion or blocking, obtain a copy, and complain to a regulator. Email princeezekiel.limboc@gmail.com to make a request. We may ask for information reasonably needed to verify it.

In the Philippines, see the National Privacy Commission’s guide to data subject rights and complaint procedure.

Children and changes

Honeycomb is a general-purpose tool. This site is not directed at children under 13 or under the applicable age of digital consent. Updates to this policy will appear here with a revised date.
